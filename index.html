About

I am a lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Zhejiang University of Science and Technology. I hold a Ph.D. in Artificial Intelligence from the University of Wollongong, Australia, where my work addressed reinforcement learning for multi-agent systems in open and dynamic environments.

Before returning to academia I worked as a software engineer at State Street and, earlier, at Taobao. That background shapes how I work today: most of my projects sit at the point where machine learning has to survive contact with real industrial data — GPU cluster benchmarking, large language models in newsroom workflows, and quality-inspection systems for manufacturing and biotech.
